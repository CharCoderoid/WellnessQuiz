Reach Out

Look through an online community whether that is a Discord server, Instagram, Reddit etc and randomly reach out with a compliment!

Ideas

  • Comment on a picture of them saying "Wow! This is really cool!"
  • DM someone from a Discord server you don't know saying something like "Hi! I know I don't know you, but I am so proud of how far you have come!"
  • Make a public post that is encouraging others with something like an inspirational quote
Remember be respectful! This is meant to help them not harm, so if they don't want to talk, just leave them be. Go again
