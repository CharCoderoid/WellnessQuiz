Invite to chat!

Reach out to them and ask if they would like to hangout! This can be as simple as a walk in the park or going to a local cafe to grab a coffee/drink. You never know how much they need it and it will help them feel less alone!

Ideas

  • Comment on their post with a "Wow! So cute!"
  • Send a message telling them 5 things that you appreciate about them
  • Make them a custom playlist of songs that remind you of them
  • Create a digital artwork or make a character of how you see them
  • Give them a call and just chat!
Remember be respectful! This is meant to help them not harm
