Help them out

Next time they are away from the place, help them out by cleaning their room or making them food or whatever they might need like grocerties. You can even leave encouraging notes in their room. You never know how much they need it and it can just simply brighten their day!

Ideas

  • Make their bed
  • Buy groceries or things they have expressed they needed for them
  • Leave an encouraging note with a pen and paper for them
  • Make them breakfast, lunch, and/or dinner
