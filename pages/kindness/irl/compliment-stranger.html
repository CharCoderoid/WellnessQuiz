Compliment Someone

Whenever you are just walking down the street or simply people watching, approach a stranger RESPECTFULLY and give them a compliment and make it genuine. It can be as simple as saying you like something they have or uplift them. You never know what someone is going through and how much they might need it

Ideas

  • I really like your outfit!
  • I know I don't know you, but I am so proud of how far you have come!
  • Wow! You are just so gorgeous!
Remember be respectful! This is meant to help them not harm, so if they aren't free or don't want to talk, just leave them be.
