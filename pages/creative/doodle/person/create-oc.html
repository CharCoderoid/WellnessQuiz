Create an OC

Grab a piece of paper or find an empty page in a notebook/journal and create a new OC based on something random. It can range from personifying an object in your room or grab three random traits and make it into someone.

Ideas

  • Personify an object in your room
  • Create your alter ego
  • Listen to a song and create a character based on it
  • Create yourself if you were in your favorite story
Do not worry about making it perfect! Just draw the best you can! :) Go again
