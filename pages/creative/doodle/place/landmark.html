Country From Memory

Grab a piece of paper or find an empty page in a notebook/journal, think of a famous landmark from a foreign country without looking it up, and draw that famous landmark from memory. If you have no recollection or any idea what it looks like, just draw how you view it.

Do not worry about making it perfect! Just draw the best you can! :) Go again
