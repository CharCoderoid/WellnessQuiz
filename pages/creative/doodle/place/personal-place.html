Perspective Draw

Grab a piece of paper or find an empty page in a notebook/journal, go to a local place (like a local cafe, a park, your home etc) and pick a spot if possible, and draw the perspective of that spot. You might notice things that you didn't notice before

Do not worry about making it perfect! Just draw the best you can! :)
