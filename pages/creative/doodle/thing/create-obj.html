Create Something

Grab a piece of paper or find an empty page in a notebook/journal, and draw an item you would like to have. This can be something real that you want or something you wish existed or something magical that might help you in your life. You can even draw the item you wish you have but with other features that may not be possible in real life.

Ideas

  • An item on your wishlist but add features that do not exist that you would like
  • Create your own pencil that has special abilities
  • Create a magical wand that is just for you
Do not worry about making it perfect! Just draw the best you can! :)
