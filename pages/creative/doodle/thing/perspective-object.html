Perspective Object

Grab a piece of paper or find an empty page in a notebook/journal, find an object near you and draw that item from the perspective you are looking at it from. Bonus points if you add some shading to it based on the lighting of where you are at. Bonus bonus points if you do a spinning 3D animation of it by simply drawing it from all perspectives.

Do not worry about making it perfect! Just draw the best you can! :)
