Sci-Fi

Write a story based around a science topic you find fascinating! This can be a way to teach the subject or just a way to tell a fun story. It doesn't have to be scientifically accurate as you are free to play around the what ifs.

Ideas

  • Life if we lived in space
  • Personifying plants
  • Chemicals if they could talk and trying to combine
