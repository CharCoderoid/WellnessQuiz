The Untold Story

Unless you personally know someone famous, chances are their story is unknown but if they want to, write a story about their life, what they've gone through etc. Sit them down for an interview and have a deep talk with them to understand them. Remember to take notes so you don't forget and use them to bring out a story that reflects their life and ask them for any feedback. If it's not possible to reach out, write with what you know/have or write a story based on how you see them as.

Do not worry about making it sound good! Just write! :)
