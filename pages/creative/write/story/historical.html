Historical Perspective

Find a historical event that you are interested in and research a perspective of someone living through it and tell their story. How did they react to certain things happening? Why? What contributions did they make? You can also branch out and include two opposing sides perspectives to get a wider range of what it was like dealing with whatever was happening

Do not worry about making it sound good! Just write! :) Go again
