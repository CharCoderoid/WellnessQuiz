Dream Romance

Write a story based on your dream romance! What would be the ideal love story you would want? What problems might come with it? If you already have a partner, write your story out or play out how it would be if the scenarios were different (met at a different time, different place etc). Use your favorite trope or ship and make it as adventurous as you want it to

Do not worry about making it sound good! Just write! :)
