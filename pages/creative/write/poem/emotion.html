Feelings

Grab a piece of paper or find an empty page in a notebook/journal and write a poem about an intense or meaningful emotion whether good or bad. It can be about a time you faced that feeling or just what happens when you that feeling comes up. What thoughts do you have? How does your body feel? Writing it out can help get the emotion out and help process it all

Do not worry about making it sound good! Just write! :) Go again
