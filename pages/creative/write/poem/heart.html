Head vs Heart

Grab a piece of paper or find an empty page in a notebook/journal and write a poem about an ongoing battle you have with your head and heart, something you want but you can't have. This can be like a career path you want but it does not seem realistic to you. What do you want? What is stopping you? How does it make you feel?

Do not worry about making it sound good! Just write! :) Go again
