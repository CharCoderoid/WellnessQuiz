Nature's Imagery

Find a quiet place and put on some calming music. Grab a piece of paper or find an empty page in a notebook/journal and write a poem using sensory detal of the natural world to describe the current mood. It can be rewritting your current mood with nature or a poetic story that involves nature. Pretend to immerse yourself in the natural environment

Do not worry about making it sound good! Just write! :)
