Item In Nature

Think of an object in nature or look outside and pick out the first thing you see. Grab a piece of paper or find an empty page in a notebook/journal and use that object as the main focus of your poem. It can be a symbol for something deeper or personify that object living in nature

Do not worry about making it sound good! Just write! :)
