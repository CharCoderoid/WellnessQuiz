Childhood Meetup

Grab a piece of paper or find an empty page in a notebook/journal and write a poem about meeting your childhood self. What would be different? What would be the same? Reflect on the ways you have grown for the better and advice you wish you could give yourself to make things easier. Write it like you were actually meeting up with them or randomly encountering them

Do not worry about making it sound good! Just write! :)
