Photo Story

Whether it's by yourself or with other people, have a fun photo session that highlights people. It can be candid moments while you are hanging out with others or set up a scene with what you have that showcases a "character" and tells a story

Ideas

  • A photo of yourself in your room dressed up as a character
  • Candid photo of your friend(s) at a Cafe
