Scribble Collage

Grab two pieces of paper and on one of them use any sort of tools you have such as pens, pencils, crayons etc and just scribble as messy as you can around it. Don't worry about making it look nice. Then, cut that up into shapes/strips and rearrange those pieces on the second piece of paper to make it look like something (landscape, vision board etc). Feel free to add texture by scrunching the paper or layering them. Glue on the pieces to the paper and enjoy the self-made collage!.

Do not worry about making it perfect! Just create and have fun! :) Go again
