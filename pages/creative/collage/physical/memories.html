Memory Collage

Grab one or more pieces of paper or a blank notebook and the photos you have and make a collage that highlights the memory of something special from those photos. This can be a memory of your friends, a particular travel trip etc. You can even write texts that highlights something from the particular moment in the photos! It is nice to have to reminisce later

Ideas

  • Using one paper, cut out the photos of your friends/family/partner to highlight the people that are special to you
  • Using a blank notebook, create a scrapbook and write a short caption of what is special about each photo
  • A nice timeline of each photo with a short caption that describes that moment
Do not worry about making it perfect! Just create and have fun! :)
