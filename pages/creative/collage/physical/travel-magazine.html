Travel Magazine

Grab several pieces of paper or a blank notebook and the photos you have and create a travel magazine highlighting the places in the photos! Whether they are places you've been or just photos you happen to have, add comments to highlight what you've learnt about that place, your memories of that place, or simply just the certain vibe of it!

Ideas

  • Make it like a travel review to show the good and the bad
  • A spread with simply places that have a similar vibe
  • A timeline highlighting places you've been and a short title that encapsulates your memory with it
Do not worry about making it perfect! Just create and have fun! :) Go again
