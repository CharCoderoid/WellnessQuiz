New Landscape Collage

Grab a piece of paper or a blank page from a notebook and using the photos you have, cut them out to make a new landscape. This can be as simple as cutting out certain parts of the image and rearranging it or combining multiple photos to create a new landscape entirely. It doesn't have to make sense if you don't want it to

Do not worry about making it perfect! Just create and have fun! :)
