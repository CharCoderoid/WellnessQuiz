Vision Board

Grab a piece of paper or a blank page in your notebook and the photos you have and create a vision board based on the vibes of what you have. This can be something that represents your goals in your career, personal growth, relationship etc. It also helps with self reflection and motivation to work towards something. When you are done, put it somewhere you frequently see so it helps motivate you!

Ideas

  • Set your vibe with things that represent you
  • Dream place to live
  • Career Objective
Do not worry about making it perfect! Just create and have fun! :)
