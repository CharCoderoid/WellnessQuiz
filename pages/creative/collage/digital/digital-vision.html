Vision Board

Using whatever photo-editing software you have on whatever device, find photos online to represent a goal you would like to achieve, whether that is career, health, travel etc and make something that will help motivate you to achieve the goal! You can envision what you want to look like when you achieve it or things that will remind you of it. If you can, print it out and put it somewhere you frequently see so it helps motivates you or set it as your wallpaper on your device. You got this!

Ideas

  • A future look into your ideal dream career
  • Photos of other people working out to motivate you to work out more
  • Your dream vacation spot and what you would like to do there
Do not worry about making it perfect! Just create and have fun! :) Go again
