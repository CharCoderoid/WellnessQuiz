Mash Collage

Using whatever photo-editing software you have on whatever device, find different kinds of photos such as a photo of a person and a photo of a landscape, and cut them out to create a new scene

Ideas

  • Your favorite celebrity in a movie/show they're not in
  • An animal shooting lasers out of their eyes with human legs
  • A whole new place with a real famous landmark
Do not worry about making it perfect! Just create and have fun! :)
